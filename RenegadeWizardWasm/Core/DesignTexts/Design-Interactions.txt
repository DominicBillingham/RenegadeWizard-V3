This outlines how the game works.

### Overview
Each turn, a player enters a string.
This string is then parsed into an ACTION and the TARGETS
An ACTION is then further broken down into a seris of EFFECTS
EFFECTS are then the smallest unit of logic the game functions on, which modify the 

### Actions
Each action has a series of targets and effects strung together.
A KICK action is A DAMAGE effect and a KNOCKBACK effect.
Each action starts with a single dice pool roll, to determine it's overall impact.
For example, a kick might only knockback, rather than knockback and stun.

### Effects
Effects fundamentally modify the state of creatures.
They are the smallest "thing" that can happen.
Often they involve changing the state of a creautre.
Effects centralise logic inside of themselves.
Effects also consider the tags on a creature.
EXAMPLE: A KNOCKBACK effect against a creature with the TAG IMMOVABLE wont' do anything.

### Tags
Tags are just a list of enums on a creature for now.
They influence the logic of an effect.
Logic is centralised in the effects rather than the tag to avoid distrubted logic.
